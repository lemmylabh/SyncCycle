# Pricing Model Guidance for Syncycle

Your pricing structure is already solid conceptually 👍 (a **freemium +
premium + B2B model**). The key now is making it **clear, persuasive,
and visually balanced** in your pricing section.

Below are the **important improvements and things to keep in mind for
your specific pricing model.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1️⃣ Limit the number of visible plans on the page

Right now you have **3 plans**, which is perfect:

-   **Free**
-   **Syncycle Plus**
-   **Corporate Wellness**

But visually you should structure them like this:

    Free           Syncycle Plus ⭐           Corporate

Always **highlight the middle plan** because that is the one you want
most people to choose.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2️⃣ Clarify the price range

Your premium price shows a **range**:

    €3.99 - €5.99 / month
    €29.99 - €39.99 / year

This can confuse users.

Better options:

### Option A (simpler)

    €4.99 / month
    €34.99 / year

### Option B (two tiers)

    Plus – €3.99
    Pro – €5.99

If the price varies later, explain **why** (features, region,
promotions, etc.).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3️⃣ Improve the feature phrasing

Features should focus on **benefits**, not technical words.

### Your current version

    Advanced AI Insights
    Detailed Analytics

### Better version

    Personalized cycle insights
    Daily energy & performance guidance
    Advanced wellness analytics
    Monthly health reports
    Ad-free experience

This connects directly to the **problem your product solves**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4️⃣ Keep feature lists consistent

Each plan should have roughly the **same number of bullets (4--6)**.

Example:

### Free

    ✓ Basic cycle tracking
    ✓ Habit tracking
    ✓ Limited AI insights
    ✓ Educational content
    ✓ Ads & sponsored content

### Syncycle Plus

    ✓ Advanced AI insights
    ✓ Personalized recommendations
    ✓ Detailed wellness analytics
    ✓ Monthly wellness reports
    ✓ Ad-free experience

### Corporate

    ✓ Team wellness dashboard
    ✓ Group health insights
    ✓ Monthly wellness reports
    ✓ Wellness workshops
    ✓ Dedicated support

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5️⃣ Make CTAs different for each plan

Your CTAs are good but can be slightly clearer.

  Plan        CTA
  ----------- ---------------------
  Free        **Start for Free**
  Premium     **Upgrade to Plus**
  Corporate   **Contact Sales**

Corporate plans rarely use **"Get Started"**, they usually use **Contact
/ Book Demo**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6️⃣ Add a recommended badge

Highlight the premium plan visually.

Example:

    Syncycle Plus
    ⭐ Most Popular

This alone can increase conversions by **20--40%**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7️⃣ Show the annual savings

For subscription products this is important.

Example:

    €4.99 / month
    or €34.99 yearly
    Save 30%

This nudges people toward yearly plans.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8️⃣ Clarify the corporate pricing

Corporate buyers need **clear expectations**.

Example:

    €3 – €5 / employee / month
    Custom pricing depending on company size

Add a small note:

    For companies with 20+ employees

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9️⃣ Add trust signals under pricing

Right under the section add small reassurance lines:

    ✓ Cancel anytime
    ✓ Secure payments
    ✓ GDPR compliant

Especially important since you're dealing with **health data**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🔟 Keep the layout clean

A clean pricing card layout should look like this:

    PLAN NAME
    Short description

    PRICE

    Feature list
    ✓
    ✓
    ✓

    CTA BUTTON

Never mix the order.

# 🎨 One design tip for your landing page

Since your page uses **glassmorphism cards**, your pricing cards should
use the **same style**:

    rounded-2xl
    bg-white/[0.08]
    backdrop-blur-md
    border border-white/15

This keeps the **visual language consistent**.
