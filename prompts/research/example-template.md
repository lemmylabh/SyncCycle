# Research Template — Delete or rename this file

This is an example of how to structure a research file.
Name files with a number prefix to control load order (e.g. 01-luteal.md, 02-follicular.md).

## Example: Luteal Phase
- Progesterone peaks around day 21 then drops sharply before menstruation
- PMS symptoms typically intensify 5–7 days before period onset
- Magnesium deficiency is linked to increased cramp severity and mood dips
- Carbohydrate cravings increase due to serotonin fluctuations driven by progesterone

## Example: Follicular Phase
- Rising estrogen improves insulin sensitivity and energy availability
- Strength training gains are maximised in this phase due to anabolic hormonal environment
- Cognitive sharpness and verbal fluency tend to peak mid-follicular
